GymPal

Your training. On your phone.

A quiet gym companion that plans the session, keeps the sets and asks for nothing in return. No sign-up at the door, no signal required, no data leaving the device — basements, busy gyms and flights all work the same.

  • Every screen works in airplane mode
  • No account — open it and train
  • History stays on your phone, not on a server

Offline by default

The whole app is on the phone. Plans, logging and history work with no signal, because nothing is waiting on a server.

No account, no wall

Open it and start. No email, no password, nothing to sign into and nothing to recover later.

Private because it can be

Workout data never leaves the device — no analytics SDKs, no ad trackers, no cloud backup you didn't ask for.

More to come.

Every app under Disconnect follows the same three rules: offline, account-free, and yours.
